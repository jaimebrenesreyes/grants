How it works

A full application, step by step

    We talk through your project, timeline and funding goals.

  2. Funder fit

  3. Writing & coordination

    You supply the research. I draft, budget and chase the letters.

  5. Submit & report

    Compliance checks before the deadline, and reports once funded.

About

A decade of grant writing and multidisciplinary research at Western University

I was a Research Associate at Western's Financial Wellness Lab (2023–2025). I co-wrote its successful Ontario Research Fund proposal, matching researchers with industry partners and collecting their letters of support, and led the writing and coordination of an NSERC Alliance application. Earlier, I worked with the Principal Investigator on an SSHRC Partnership Grant application for a partnership spanning Canada, El Salvador, the Netherlands and Belgium, including the literature review, the theoretical framework and the budget.

Outside the university, I'm Board Member and Secretary of Heart Links Lazos de Corazón, a London nonprofit, and sit on its Fundraising and Engagement Committee. I also teach: this year I led workshops helping graduate students build their own online portfolios.

Ph.D. in Hispanic Studies, Western University · English and Spanish · reading knowledge of Italian and Portuguese
