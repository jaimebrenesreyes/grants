About

Years of grant writing at Western University

I was a Research Associate at Western's Financial Wellness Lab (2023–2025). I co-wrote its successful Ontario Research Fund proposal in six weeks, matching researchers with industry partners and collecting their letters of support, and led the writing and coordination of an NSERC Alliance application. Earlier, I co-wrote a SSHRC Partnership Grant for a partnership spanning Canada, El Salvador, the Netherlands and Belgium, carrying it from Letter of Intent to award.

Outside the university, I'm Board Member and Secretary of Heart Links Lazos de Corazón, a London nonprofit, and sit on its Fundraising and Engagement Committee. I also teach: this year I led workshops helping graduate students build their own online portfolios.
